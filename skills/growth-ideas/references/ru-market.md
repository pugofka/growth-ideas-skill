# The Russian market: what to account for in growth hypotheses

A reference, not a checklist: take from it only what applies to the product in the context. Legislation and service availability in Russia change often — check everything marked ⚠️ for currency before launching a hypothesis, and warn the owner about it.

## Search and analytics

- Account for **both** search engines: Yandex and Google. Demand and rankings differ between them; a "Google-only" hypothesis for Russian B2B is incomplete.
- Demand and query wording are checked in **Yandex Wordstat**; without a Wordstat or Search Console export do not state search volumes — give a list of queries to check instead.
- Data: **Yandex Metrica** (goals, traffic sources, Webvisor session replay), **Yandex Webmaster** (indexing, queries, site region), **Google Search Console**.
- For local presence and trust: **Yandex Business / Yandex Maps** and **2GIS** — reviews and the business listing affect both search and conversion.

## Acquisition channels

- Paid: **Yandex Direct**, **VK Ads**, **Telegram Ads**, sponsored posts in Telegram channels.
- Content and communities: **Telegram channels** (the main messenger and media channel for B2B), **VK communities**, **Dzen**; for IT and digital — **Habr** and **vc.ru**.
- Directories and ratings: for studios and agencies — industry ratings and contractor directories; for software and integrators — **partner directories and app marketplaces of the platforms their customers use** (CMS, CRM, e-commerce platforms). These bring inbound leads without cold outreach.
- Services for small businesses — also **Avito Services** and niche aggregators.
- ⚠️ Services of foreign companies may be unavailable, blocked, or legally risky to advertise on (for example, Meta's platforms). Do not build a hypothesis on a channel without checking its status.

## Law and compliance

Checked on 2026-10-15. Two different requirements are often mixed up: **ad marking** applies to ad placements on the internet, while **consent** applies to promotional messages sent to people. Name the right one in each hypothesis.

- ⚠️ **Ad marking for online placements** (Article 18.1 of Federal Law 38-FZ "On Advertising"): an ad placed on the internet must carry an identifier (erid), and its data goes to the Unified Register of Internet Advertising (ERIR) through an advertising data operator (ORD). This covers Yandex Direct, VK Ads, Telegram Ads, sponsored posts in Telegram channels, placements with bloggers and on partner sites. A hypothesis with a paid or sponsored placement must include marking in its plan and cost.
  - **Email and push mailings are outside Article 18.1**: Roskomnadzor's letter of 24.10.2023 No. 03-97847 says the marking requirements do not apply to ads sent to email addresses or as push messages to groups of users. Do not add erid or ERIR reporting to an email or push campaign. Source: [1C:ITS summary of the letter](https://its.1c.ru/db/content/newsclar/src/486832.htm).
  - SMS and messenger mailings: whether marking applies was not checked against a primary source — verify before relying on either answer.
- ⚠️ **Consent for promotional messages** (Article 18 of 38-FZ): advertising over telecom networks — email, SMS, messengers, calls — is allowed only with the recipient's prior consent. The sender must be able to prove the consent, and must stop on the recipient's request. A purchase is not consent to advertising. Personal business emails without advertising are a different matter, but the line is thin. Source: [Article 18, 38-FZ](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_58968/f892dec1383709792452f18d36e7043306e2be0a/).
- ⚠️ **Personal data (Federal Law 152-FZ)**: consent to processing in every data collection form, a published personal data processing policy, storage of Russian citizens' data in Russia. Any hypothesis that collects contacts (forms, popups, lead magnets, quizzes) must include consent.
- ⚠️ Analytics counters on the site may require cookie consent — account for this when measuring results (some leads will not be visible in analytics).
- ⚠️ Restrictions on mentioning and advertising certain categories of persons and resources (foreign agents, banned organizations) — check when a hypothesis involves external platforms and bloggers.

## The B2B buyer in Russia

- **Referrals and concrete case studies** decide: "who, what was done, what changed". A case study with numbers and the client's name beats any landing page.
- **Trust in the company**: legal details on the site (legal entity, INN/OGRN registration numbers), a clear contract, invoice-based payment. For small businesses — a live contact in Telegram.
- Small businesses more often choose between your product and **"do nothing" / "do it ourselves"** than between you and a competitor.
- Data concerns: handing the customer base and sales data to a third-party service is a frequent blocker. A strong hypothesis removes it through how the offer is structured, not through persuasion.

## Seasonality and calendar

- Business activity drops: **New Year holidays (early January)** and **May holidays**. Do not launch or evaluate tests during these weeks.
- Account for the product's industry seasonality (e.g., sales and the pre-New Year peak in e-commerce; the school year for education and school services).
